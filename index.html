Frequently asked questions

Why won't Live mode start without headphones?

Playing your delayed voice through the speaker would echo and squeal. Ossiato only plays feedback into headphones — wired, USB or Bluetooth.

Which headphones are best?

Wired headphones (for example EarPods) add the least delay. AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones work too; Ossiato measures their delay and shows the delay you actually hear.

Can Ossiato change the sound of real phone calls?

No. iOS does not allow apps to process real phone calls. That is why Ossiato includes a call trainer where you rehearse calls with a simulated caller.

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. Audio is processed on your iPhone. Practice recordings and speech metrics stay on the device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel Ossiato Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ossiato Pro. Restore Purchases is in the app's Settings and on the paywall.

Is Ossiato a medical device?

No. Ossiato is a practice tool, not a medical device, and it does not replace working with a speech-language professional.
